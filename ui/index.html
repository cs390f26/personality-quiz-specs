True Colors Personality Quiz

Welcome!

This personality quiz is based on the True Colors test. You will answer 10 multiple-choice questions to learn which personality category best matches your responses.

Each answer contributes points toward one of four personality categories: Blue, Green, Gold, or Orange.

How it works

  • Enter your name before completing the quiz.
  • Answer all 10 multiple-choice questions.
  • Submit the quiz to view your personality result.
  • View overall statistics from completed quiz attempts.
